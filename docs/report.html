What NEON keeps from each trap

A pitfall sample is sorted into carabids, other invertebrates, reptiles and amphibians, and small mammals. A subset of carabids is pinned; the rest, and all the bycatch, is preserved in ethanol, first as one vial per trap (trap sorting) and then pooled per plot and bout for long-term storage (archive pooling). The Biorepository holds these as separate collections.

Reptiles and amphibians in the traps

6,149 of 6,149 herptile bycatch photographs were segmented, giving 9,021 outlines from 3,072 records at 41 sites. Most vials were photographed twice, once from each side, so an animal usually appears as two outlines. Each photo has a metric ruler in frame, so every outline has a millimetre scale. Length here is the longest path along the body's midline: total length including the tail for salamanders and lizards, and out to the toes for a frog with a leg extended. It is not snout-vent length.

There are no human outlines to compare with, but each record states how many animals the vial holds. The number of outlines in a photo equals that count on 91.5% of 6,147 photos and is within one on 99.0%; it is higher on 7.4% (detached limbs and tails outlined separately) and lower on 1.1%. 48 outlines are speckled, failed masks and are flagged.

Species suggestions from GBIF

3,269 of 3,430 herptile records are already identified to species. For the other 161, GBIF was asked which species of the recorded genus or family people have observed within 100 km of the site, and BioCLIP 2 scored each segmented animal against those candidates. 64 records get a suggestion: 29 because only one candidate species occurs nearby, and 35 because the image model is at least 60% sure among several. These are suggestions for a curator, not determinations.

How far to trust them, from running the same procedure where the species is known: when only one candidate occurs nearby it is the recorded species on 99.8% of 1,601 records. When there are several candidates the image model alone is weak on preserved specimens, and worth using only when it is confident (table below).

How well it works

Finding specimens in tray photos

On 577 tray photos from 2018, the pipeline found 11,949 specimens against 11,655 human boxes: precision 0.971, recall 0.996 at IoU 0.5, and the count matched exactly on 75% of trays. Those boxes began as machine proposals that people corrected, which flatters a detector of the same family. The independent check is the volunteers' elytra lines: 99.6% of 10,183 annotated individuals have a predicted mask under the line.

Limitations

  • Images, not specimens, set the coverage. About 1% of pinned vouchers at the Biorepository have a photo.
  • 8,807 specimens have no millimetre scale (Biorepository grid-backdrop photos, most domain-office photos, the 2016 individual photos, and rulers the tick reader rejected). Their measurements are in pixels.
  • Elytra length reads long. Against people's lines the predicted elytra length is about 8 to 10% too long (0.8 mm on the 2018 ethanol specimens), consistently, with a high correlation. Treat absolute elytra lengths as biased high; comparisons within a dataset are much better than that.
  • The part model was trained on pinned specimens. It is applied to ethanol specimens as well; the validation table shows what that costs.
  • Herptile lengths are midline lengths of outlines, not snout-vent lengths, each animal is usually outlined twice (two photos per vial), and the species suggestions are a curator's starting point, not identifications.
  • Sentinel scale rests on an assumption: that each ruler crop has the same resolution as the specimen crops from the same tray photo. Sentinel specimens also cannot be linked to NEON sites.
  • 225 specimens used a fallback prompt (no detection or incomplete part labels); check det_source.
  • Quality flags are not a review. 4,601 masks have solidity below 0.5 and 7,556 specimens have a body cut by the photo's edge. Nobody inspected the masks one by one.
  • The 2016 individual photos are the weakest pool. They are crops about 110 by 190 pixels with no scale; 640 of 5,668 have no elytra found, and some outlines are plainly wrong.
  • Ventral and lateral photos are segmented like dorsal ones; filter on view before using their measurements.
