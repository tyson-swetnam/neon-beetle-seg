# Validation

Numbers from the v0.2.0 run (2026-10-06). They are regenerated by `nbs validate` and stored in the
lake as `validation_summary`, `validation_detection` and `validation_traits`; the SAM 3 comparison
is `sam3_comparison`, the herp identification check is `herp_id_validation`.

## Finding beetles in tray photos

577 photos of ethanol specimens from 2018, compared with two independent human references.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Specimens found | 11,949 |
| Precision against 11,655 human boxes (IoU >= 0.5) | 0.971 |
| Recall against the human boxes | 0.996 |
| Trays where the count equals the number of human boxes | 75% of 577 |
| Annotated individuals with a predicted mask under their elytra line | 99.6% of 10,183 |
| Trays where the count equals the number of annotated individuals | 77% of 495 |

Two cautions about the references:

- The human boxes were exported from CVAT with `source="file"`: they started as machine proposals
  that people then corrected. Agreement with a Grounding DINO detector is therefore optimistic.
- The elytra lines are fully independent of any detector, so "annotated individuals covered" is
  the stricter test. It uses only lines whose pixel frame matches the photo (see below), which is
  10,183 individuals on 495 trays.

343 of the 11,949 detections (2.9%) match no human box. They were not examined one by one; the
pilot saw detached legs counted as specimens, and that is the likely cause for some of them.

### Crowded trays

Whole-photo detection alone gave recall 0.953: 527 of the 547 missed specimens were in 19 trays
with recall below 0.8, the worst being a tray of 266 small beetles where one was found. A tiled
second pass now takes over when it finds clearly more specimens (25 of 577 trays). On the six
worst trays that lifted recall from 0.41 to 0.98; on ordinary trays tiling is slightly worse
(precision 0.935 against 0.985 on a sample of 14), which is why it is a fallback and not the
default.

### SAM 3 comparison

`facebook/sam3`, prompted with the word "beetle", on 100 of the same trays at 2,048 px:

| Method | Specimens found | Precision | Recall | Annotated individuals covered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grounding DINO + SAM 2.1 (this pipeline) | 2,153 | 0.974 | 0.992 | 98.7% |
| SAM 3, text prompt | 1,903 | 0.968 | 0.871 | 86.1% |

SAM 3 matched the pipeline on ordinary trays (recall 0.993 on the 97 trays with fewer than 60
beetles) and fell to 0.40 on the 3 crowded ones, the same failure whole-photo Grounding DINO
has. It ran at 1.6 s per tray with a 3.7 GiB peak. No measurement in the lake comes from SAM 3.

## Measurements against human annotations

Each predicted value is compared with the line a person drew on the same specimen. Bias is
predicted minus human.

| Dataset | Measurement | Specimens | Bias (mm) | Mean abs. error (mm) | Median abs. error | Within 10% | Pearson r |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 ethanol trays | Elytra length, midline | 10,045 | +0.79 | 0.80 | 8.0% | 63% | 0.984 |
| 2018 ethanol trays | Elytra length, full extent | 10,144 | +0.90 | 0.90 | 9.1% | 56% | 0.985 |
| 2018 ethanol trays | Elytra width at base | 10,040 | +0.44 | 0.52 | 6.0% | 66% | 0.941 |
| Hawaii pinned | Elytra length, midline | 1,593 | +0.27 | 0.27 | 9.2% | 54% | 0.911 |
| Hawaii pinned | Elytra length, full extent | 1,600 | +0.30 | 0.30 | 10.4% | 48% | 0.914 |
| Hawaii pinned | Elytra maximum width | 1,600 | +0.08 | 0.08 | 2.0% | 88% | 0.897 |
| Hawaii pinned | Pronotum basal width | 1,592 | +0.07 | 0.10 | 11.1% | 45% | 0.764 |

What to take from this:

- **Elytra length reads long, consistently.** The correlation is high and the fitted slope is
  1.05 to 1.09, so the error is a steady over-read rather than scatter: about 0.8 mm on the 2018
  beetles (median human elytra length 8.75 mm) and 0.27 mm on the much smaller Hawaiian ones
  (median 2.42 mm). The part model labels a strip in front of where annotators start their line
  and reaches the very tip of the apex. Treat absolute elytra lengths as biased high by roughly
  8 to 10%; relative comparisons within a dataset are much better than that.
- **Widths are good.** Maximum elytra width on pinned specimens is within 2% at the median.
- **Pronotum basal width is the weakest.** The median human value is 0.77 mm.
- **Compare like with like.** The 2018 volunteers drew elytra width at the base of the elytra,
  not at the widest point: against their lines, predicted *maximum* width reads about 30% larger.
  `elytra_base_width_mm` is the comparable column for that dataset, `elytra_width_mm` for Hawaii.
- **The part model was trained on pinned specimens** and is applied to ethanol specimens too. It
  found elytra on 98% of the 2018 specimens and its length agreement there is no worse than on
  pinned specimens.

There are no human measurements for the Biorepository pinned photos, the 2016 individual photos
or the sentinel crops, so their accuracy is not measured. The pinned photos are the same kind of
image as Hawaii's; the 2016 individual photos are much lower resolution (about 110 x 190 px).

## Scale

| Scale reader | Checked against | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Ruler ticks | Hawaii's human 1 cm scale bars, 40 photos | read on 35; all within 0.6% (ratio 0.996 to 1.006) |
| Checkerboard | Zooniverse scale bars from three frame-consistent workflows, 35 photos | within 0.6% on average for two workflows (ratio 0.994), 2.9% for the third |
| Printed bar + OCR | internal consistency | bar lengths fall on the photo series' magnifications: 717 px = 5 mm at 1x, 1,077 px at 1.5x, 1,435 px at 2x, and 717 px = 1 mm at 5x |

Coverage: 574 of 577 2018 trays, all 417 Hawaii trays, 42,668 of 44,510 sentinel crops, all 6,149
herp photos, and 826 of 2,085 Biorepository pinned photos (789 of 1,232 in the main voucher
collection; the grid-backdrop series and most domain-office photos have no readable bar). The
2016 individual photos have no scale in frame.

The printed-bar reader has no independent ground truth. The sentinel scale assumes each ruler
crop has the same resolution as the specimen crops from the same tray photo.

### The 2018 annotation frame problem

`BeetleMeasurements.csv` gives each line in the pixels of a resized image, with the resized
size in `resized_image_dim`. For Zooniverse workflows 21840, 22073 and 23248 that holds: lines
land on their specimens and the annotated scale bar agrees with the checkerboard. For workflows
21652 and 21827 it does not: the annotated scale bar is 9 to 22% shorter than the checkerboard
says it should be, by a different amount in each photo, and the lines land beside their
specimens. Before this was handled, only 26 to 33% of those workflows' lines fell on a specimen.

Consequences here: the tray scale comes from the checkerboard, never from the annotation; and
only lines from the three consistent workflows are paired with specimens. `dist_cm` itself is
unaffected in all workflows, because a line and its scale bar share a frame.

## Herptile bycatch

6,149 photographs of 3,072 records gave 9,021 outlines. Most vials were photographed twice, once
from each side, so an animal usually appears as two outlines.

**Finding the animals.** There are no human outlines to compare with, but each record states how
many animals its vial holds:

| Outlines in the photo vs animals on the record | Share of 6,147 photos |
|---|---|
| Equal | 91.5% |
| Within one | 99.0% |
| More outlines than animals | 7.4% |
| Fewer | 1.1% |

Extra outlines are expected to be detached limbs and tails, which preserved amphibians shed
easily; they were not examined one by one. 48 outlines are speckled, failed masks
(`largest_blob_frac` below 0.8). All 6,149 photos had their ruler read; nothing independent checks
that scale, but the values are consistent within each photo series (about 23 to 27 pixels per
millimetre for the 3,200 px photos).

**Length.** `midline_length_mm` is the longest path along the outline's skeleton: total length
with the tail for salamanders and lizards, and out to the toes for a frog with a leg extended. It
is not snout-vent length, and no human measurement exists to compare it with. Medians are 52 mm
for amphibian outlines and 48 mm for reptiles.

**Species suggestions.** 3,269 of 3,430 records are already identified to species. The procedure
for the other 161 (GBIF candidates within 100 km, then BioCLIP 2 among them) was run on records
whose species is known:

| Records with a known species | Records | Median candidates | Known species among the candidates | Most-observed candidate correct | BioCLIP pick correct |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All | 2,646 | 1 | 99.4% | 85.4% | 75.4% |
| One candidate nearby | 1,601 | 1 | | 99.8% | |
| Two or more candidates | 1,045 | 6 | 98.7% | 63.3% | 38.1% |
| Two or more, BioCLIP probability at least 0.6 | 298 | 2 | 98.0% | 57.4% | 73.2% |

So:

- **Range alone is nearly always right when it leaves one candidate** (99.8%), and GBIF's
  candidate list contains the true species 99% of the time.
- **BioCLIP alone is weak on preserved specimens**: 38% when it has to choose among several
  candidates, worse than picking the most commonly observed one (63%). Ethanol specimens are
  faded and bloated, and half the photos show the underside.
- **A confident BioCLIP pick is usable**: 73% correct at probability 0.6 or more.

64 of the 161 records get a suggestion under those two rules: 29 from a single nearby candidate
and 35 from a confident BioCLIP pick, the second kind right about three times in four by the
check above. 8 records have no candidate at all, and the rest are left without a suggestion.
`herp_id_suggestions` keeps the candidates and both picks for every record, so a different rule
can be applied.

## Masks

Nobody inspected the masks one by one. Spot checks of random samples from each pool found and
led to fixes for two systematic failures:

- **Backdrop masks.** On tight crops with a plain backdrop, SAM returned the backdrop instead of
  the specimen for 2,965 of the 5,668 Biorepository 2016 individual photos, 257 sentinel crops
  and 6 pinned photos. These are now detected by how much of the window's border they cover and
  replaced by their complement (`mask_fix = inverted_backdrop`; 6 could not be fixed and are
  marked `backdrop_unfixed`). Usable part labels on the 2016 photos went from 44% to 89%.
- **Crowded trays**, above.

Flags to filter on are listed in [pipeline.md](pipeline.md#quality-flags). Of 65,830 beetle
specimens, 64,102 have elytra labels, 52,967 have head, pronotum and elytra, and 57,023 have a
millimetre scale. On sentinel crops the head or pronotum is missed on about a quarter of
specimens (glossy black beetles against printed labels).
